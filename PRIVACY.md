# Privacy Policy for DilSe Music

**Effective Date:** October 19, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 19, 2026

DilSe Music ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the DilSe Music application across Android, Web, and Desktop platforms.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide
- **Google Account Information:** When you choose to sign in with Google, we access basic profile information provided by Google (such as your display name, email address, and profile photo URL) via standard OAuth 2.0 (`email`, `profile`, `openid`).
- **Playlists & Preferences:** If you import playlists (e.g. Spotify or YouTube Music), your playlist titles, track lists, and listening preferences are stored locally on your device and cached securely for session restoration.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
- **Playback & Queue State:** To offer continuous playback, audio crossfade, and smart auto-mix features, the app stores recent playback states, favorite tracks, and equal-power volume settings locally on your device.
- **Anonymous Visitor Tokens:** To provide collaborative machine-learning recommendations without requiring authentication (in Guest Mode), an anonymous YouTube Music visitor token is maintained locally.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:
- Authenticate your user session and maintain your profile across app restarts.
- Synchronize your personalized playlists, liked songs, and listening history.
- Provide continuous playback, high-fidelity audio stream resolution, and personalized song recommendations.
- Improve application performance, resolve crashes, and enhance audio quality.

We **do not** sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to third parties.

---

## 3. Data Storage & Security

- **Local Storage:** All user preferences, playback sessions, and cached playlists are stored locally on your device using encrypted, sandboxed app storage.
- **Direct Edge Streaming:** Audio streaming connections resolve directly through Cloudflare Workers and official CDN endpoints without routing private user identities through intermediary proxy servers.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

DilSe Music may interact with the following third-party APIs solely to deliver streaming and authentication features:
- **Google OAuth 2.0:** For user sign-in and profile synchronization.
- **JioSaavn & YouTube:** For public audio stream resolution and metadata retrieval.

These services govern data in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

---

## 5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You retain full control over your data:
- **Sign Out & Switch to Guest Mode:** You can disconnect your Google account or switch to Guest Mode at any time from the profile menu.
- **Clear Data:** You can reset all local playback history, cached playlists, and preferences at any time directly through the in-app Settings menu.
